Pricing rules (this device only)

Cash price = net cost × markup, rounded up so it ends in 9 (49 or 99 from £100). Finance price = cash price ÷ (1 − fee), rounded up the same way, so the finance company's fee never comes out of your margin. The Excel master holds the same rules; change them there for the permanent record.
